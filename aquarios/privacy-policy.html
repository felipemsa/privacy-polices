Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

In short

  • Your aquariums, measurements, tasks, livestock and photos are stored on your device. We do not have a server that holds your data.
  • Cloud backup is optional and goes to your own Google Drive or iCloud. We cannot read it.
  • We use Google services for ads and anonymous usage statistics, and RevenueCat to manage subscriptions.

1. Data stored on your device

Everything you create in aquariOS (aquariums, water parameters, maintenance tasks, the species you add to your aquariums, the filter and planting settings of each aquarium, and the photos you take of them) is saved locally on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes this data unless you made a backup.

Photos you take or choose are copied into the app's private storage. The app only accesses the camera or your photo library when you ask it to, and only saves a photo to your gallery when you tap "Save to gallery".

2. Optional cloud backup

  • Android (Google Drive): when you tap the connect button, you sign in with Google and authorize the app to use a private app folder in your Google Drive (appDataFolder). The app only reads and writes its own backup files there. It cannot see your other Drive files.
  • iOS (iCloud): backups are saved in the app's iCloud container in your Apple account.
  • Backups contain your app data and, if the "Include livestock photos" option is on, your livestock photos. You can turn photos off, delete only the photos or delete the whole backup from the Backup screen.
  • Floca Studios has no access to your Google or Apple credentials, your backup files or their content.

3. Third-party services

  • Google AdMob (free plan): serves ads and may collect device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID), IP address, device and OS information and usage data, according to your consent choices. AdMob policies.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase: collects anonymous usage statistics (for example, which screens are used, app version, device model, approximate region) and app events that help us improve the app. It does not receive your aquarium data, photos or backup. Firebase privacy.
  • RevenueCat (subscriptions): processes your purchase and subscription status through Google Play or the App Store, using an anonymous app user ID. We never see your payment details. RevenueCat privacy.
  • Google Sign-In / Sign in with Apple: used only to connect your own cloud storage for backup and restore.
  • Cloudflare (content delivery): the species catalog photos are downloaded from our content server on Cloudflare. As with any web request, Cloudflare processes your IP address and standard technical data to deliver the files. No personal data is sent. Cloudflare privacy.
  • Google Play Services: app runtime and essential services. Google privacy.

4. Species catalog feedback

To improve the species catalog, the app sends the following through Google Analytics for Firebase, without your name, email or account:

  • which species are viewed and added to aquariums, and the aquarium type;
  • species suggestions and error reports you choose to send, including the optional short comment you type.

Please do not include personal information (such as your name, phone or email) in suggestions or comments.

5. Notifications

Maintenance reminders are scheduled locally on your device. They are not sent through our servers.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Data on your device: deleted when you remove it in the app or uninstall the app.
  • Cloud backup: deleted from the Backup screen, or directly from your Google Drive or iCloud settings.
  • Analytics data: kept by Google according to the Google Analytics data retention settings, then deleted automatically.

7. Your privacy rights

GDPR (European Economic Area and UK)

Users in the EEA/UK see a consent dialog through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). You can change or revoke your consent at any time in the app settings.

LGPD (Brazil)

We process data in line with the Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados, respecting transparency, purpose limitation and your rights to access, correction and deletion.

US state privacy laws (CCPA / CPRA)

Residents of applicable US states can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising through the in-app privacy form.

To exercise any right, contact us at the email below.

8. Children's privacy

The app is not directed to anyone under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
